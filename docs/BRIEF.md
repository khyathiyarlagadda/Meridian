# Meridian Project Brief

## Overview
Meridian is an intelligent e-commerce analytics and insight platform designed for modern D2C merchants in India.

## Demo Merchant: NOVA
NOVA is a fictional Indian electronics and lifestyle brand selling direct-to-consumer accessories across India.

### Product Categories
- Wireless Earbuds
- Power Banks
- Phone Cases
- Smart Watches
- Chargers
- Screen Protectors
- Bluetooth Speakers

### Business Objectives
1. Identify cross-sell and bundling opportunities (e.g. Earbuds -> Phone Cases, Phone Cases + Screen Protectors).
2. Reactivate at-risk and lapsed customer cohorts (Win-back & High-Value Lapsed).
3. Monitor product lifecycle trends (Detecting emerging vs. declining SKUs).
